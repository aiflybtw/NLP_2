You are taking part in an economic decision-making study. You will make three lists of choices between two payments. In each row, Option A is a smaller amount of money paid EARLIER and Option B is a larger amount of money paid LATER. For every row, choose either Option A or Option B.

List "today_vs_1m": Option A = $X guaranteed TODAY, Option B = $80 guaranteed IN 1 MONTH
List "today_vs_6m": Option A = $X guaranteed TODAY, Option B = $80 guaranteed IN 6 MONTHS
List "6m_vs_7m": Option A = $X guaranteed IN 6 MONTHS, Option B = $80 guaranteed IN 7 MONTHS

In every list the amount $X in Option A takes these values, from the first row to the last:
Row 1: $75   Row 2: $70   Row 3: $65   Row 4: $60   Row 5: $50   Row 6: $40   Row 7: $30

How you will be paid: 1 out of every 10 participants is selected by a raffle. For each selected participant, one of his or her decisions is chosen at random and paid exactly as the participant chose. Payment is made by a money order guaranteed by the Federal Reserve Bank and is sent by mail.

Your choices are anonymous. This is a one-time study. You will not interact with any other participant and you will not receive any feedback about your choices or anyone else's.

Provide your answer in JSON format only, with exactly these keys:
"reasoning": a short explanation of how you made your choices (string),
"choices": an object with three keys "today_vs_1m", "today_vs_6m", "6m_vs_7m", each containing a list of 7 strings, "A" or "B", one per row in the order above.
